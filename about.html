I'm a UX researcher, even though the words 'researcher' or 'UX' have never appeared in my job titles. I do my best work when I'm talking to customers and learning about them.

I can conduct the research you need - and do it well. My work does the talking.

  • I've done research on copy, on icons, on bots, on IVR systems, and finance apps. I've created complete research portfolios and strategies to launch mobile apps and brand new operations systems for teams of 100+.
  • I've worked in a charter school, local government, a major corporation, and startups. I can filter jargon and have a human conversation, while keeping in mind tight regulations and sensitive data.

I can (and have) learned new industries and operating rhythms on the job, and have taught them to others. I can work with no money and no research tools at all, I can set up tools from scratch, or I can fit into existing systems.
