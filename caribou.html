Offer Selection Redesign

Caribou

Reframing the offer selection experience to make the next step feel clearer, more actionable, and easier to trust.

This page is structured as a comparison-led case study so the visual change is immediate, while the narrative and supporting data stay easy to scan below.

Before

An earlier version of the offer selection experience with heavier visual competition and a less guided decision path.

After

A more focused flow with stronger hierarchy, clearer emphasis, and a calmer experience around offer choice.

Problem

Offer selection needed stronger clarity and a more confident path forward.

Focus

Improve hierarchy, decision support, and visual emphasis around primary actions.

Audience

Members comparing options and deciding what step to take next.

Outcome

Space reserved for live metrics, qualitative feedback, or adoption lift.

Narrative

Context

This redesign centers on the moment a user has to evaluate and choose an offer. The goal is not just to make the interface look cleaner, but to make the decision feel more understandable and lower-friction.

Challenge

The earlier experience asked users to process several competing elements at once. When information hierarchy is crowded in a choice-heavy flow, even small decisions can feel heavier than they need to.

Design Approach

The redesign uses clearer emphasis, more deliberate spacing, and a calmer visual rhythm so the page guides the user toward the most important comparison and the next action without forcing extra effort.

What Changed

The after state shows a more directed experience: stronger hierarchy, less visual noise, and more intentional separation between supporting information and the primary decision path.

Project Details

  • Primary Goal Make offer selection easier to understand at a glance.
  • Design Lens Reduce cognitive load while increasing confidence in the next step.
  • Evidence Slot Ready for completion rate, drop-off, or user feedback data.
  • Key Constraint Improve clarity without losing important supporting context.
  • Team Notes Useful place for timeline, collaborators, tools, or ownership notes.

What Improved

Clearer Hierarchy

The most important information now lands first, reducing the need to search for the next step.

Simpler Decision Path

The interface better supports comparison and action instead of asking users to decode the page structure.

More Confident Experience

The overall flow feels more deliberate and supportive, which is especially important in a choice-heavy moment.
