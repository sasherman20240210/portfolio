Chat Experience Comparison

GEICO

A case study on GEICO conversational support experiences, including chatbot strategy, tailored Contact Us interactions, and guidance patterns that helped customers find the right service path more quickly.

The work combined UX review, conversational-content strategy, and analytics feedback loops to evaluate how small differences in message flow, styling, and next-step guidance shaped customer confidence.

Service Phone Number Directory

A service-directory chat flow that shows how the experience frames support pathways, pacing, and the structure of the conversation.

Payment Support

A payments-focused contact modal that highlights a similar chat pattern with different styling cues and a distinct support path.

Narrative

Context

GEICO was expanding conversational experiences across chatbot, Contact Us, voice assistant, IVR, and quote-and-sell flows. The work focused on helping application teams create more user-friendly support paths while keeping content, routing, and measurement consistent.

Challenge

Support experiences can feel fragmented when similar flows use different message patterns, visual cues, or routing logic. Customers need to understand where they are, what the system can help with, and what happens next.

Design Approach

I compared related support flows, reviewed chatbot behavior, and paired qualitative observations with Adobe Analytics signals from customer interactions. The work informed tailored Contact Us experiences and internal conversational-content guidance for customer-facing applications.

What Changed

The page can frame the two clips as evidence of a broader strategy: making support routing clearer, making conversational UI patterns more consistent, and using analytics to learn what customers were trying to accomplish.

Project Details

  • Primary Goal Improve clarity and consistency across GEICO conversational support experiences.
  • Role Senior Digital Designer / conversational UX strategy, content guidance, analytics-informed evaluation.
  • Methods Chatbot review, flow comparison, Adobe Analytics feedback loops, conversational-content guidelines, cross-application team support.
  • Systems GEICO chatbot, Contact Us modal, voice assistant, IVR, and customer-facing support applications.
  • Evidence Portfolio source files support Contact Us modal work, Adobe Analytics logging, chatbot observations, and conversational-content guidelines.

What Improved

Support Path Clarity

Tailored Contact Us interactions helped customers understand which support route matched their need instead of forcing every question through the same generic path.

Measurement Feedback

Customer inputs and chatbot observations were paired with Adobe Analytics signals to show what people were trying to do and where support flows needed refinement.

Conversation Consistency

Internal content guidelines helped teams apply clearer conversational patterns across chatbot, Alexa, Google Home, and IVR experiences.

Research Outcomes

Conversational Support Evidence

This work examined how people moved through GEICO support experiences when they needed a clear path to help. The evidence connects chatbot behavior, Contact Us routing, and analytics signals to show where conversational patterns supported customers and where consistency could improve.

Flow Review Each chat path was reviewed from entry point through completion to identify moments of friction or clarity.
Pattern Comparison The flows were compared side by side to evaluate pacing, message treatment, and next-step guidance.
Design Synthesis Findings were grouped into themes that could guide stronger consistency across support interactions.
Information Change Buttons

Support Path Visibility

The button-state evidence can be used to discuss how interface cues shape customer understanding of available support routes. This is a good place to explain whether users could tell which option applied to their situation and how the interaction reduced uncertainty.

In-Flow Tooltip

Contextual Guidance

The tooltip evidence can be used to show how small guidance moments helped curious or uncertain customers understand what the interface was asking for before they committed to a next step.

Narrative

The research focused on the parts of the chat experience that shape confidence before a user reaches a final answer. Entry cues, message rhythm, and the handoff between automated guidance and support options all affect whether the experience feels helpful or fragmented.

Looking at two flows together makes the differences easier to evaluate without turning either clip into an isolated example. The result is a clearer read on which interaction patterns should stay consistent across GEICO support moments.
