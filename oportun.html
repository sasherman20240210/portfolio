Case Study 01

Native Mobile App Experience

This case study focuses on the Oportun Native Mobile App, where customers needed a clearer way to understand their financial options, review important details, and take the next step from a small-screen experience.

Image 1

The first Oportun visual in the comparison set, highlighting the initial experience state and the details users need to understand first.

Image 2

The second Oportun visual in the comparison set, showing how the experience continues and how the information hierarchy evolves.

Experience Redesign

Narrative

Context

Oportun customers use the Native Mobile App to manage important financial tasks in moments that often require speed, clarity, and trust. The experience needed to make key information easier to find without overwhelming users.

Challenge

Mobile financial-service experiences have to balance simplicity with responsibility. Customers need plain-language guidance, visible next steps, and enough context to feel confident before taking action.

Design Approach

The app experience was evaluated through the lens of information hierarchy, task flow, and decision support. The goal was to surface what mattered most, reduce unnecessary friction, and make each screen feel easier to scan and act on.

What Changed

The updated flow puts customer needs at the center: clearer screen structure, more direct guidance, and stronger visual prioritization of key actions and supporting information.

Project Details

  • Primary Goal Improve clarity and confidence in the Oportun Native Mobile App experience.
  • Design Lens Mobile task flow, information hierarchy, customer comprehension, and trust.
  • Evidence Slot Ready for usability insights, task-completion findings, comprehension metrics, or product-performance data.
  • Key Constraint Make complex financial information feel approachable without oversimplifying important decisions.

What Improved

Clearer Mobile Flow

The experience better organizes key information so customers can understand where they are, what matters, and what to do next.

Stronger Decision Support

Important details and actions are framed in a way that helps users move forward with more confidence.

Better Small-Screen Hierarchy

The app screens prioritize the most useful content first, reducing friction in moments where clarity matters most.

Case Study 02

Location Research Evidence

This research effort examined how Oportun locations appeared in real-world map contexts. Each view is treated as equal evidence from the same study, helping frame patterns around visibility, surrounding context, and customer discovery.

In-Person Visits The research team visited each location to observe the setting and surrounding context firsthand.
Employee & Customer Interviews Each visit included conversations with employees and customers to understand practical needs and local context.
Virtual Follow-Ups Follow-up interviews helped validate what the team heard on site and clarify patterns across locations.

Narrative

The research focused on how Oportun locations show up in everyday search and map contexts, where customers may be evaluating convenience, proximity, and credibility before deciding whether to visit.

Looking across three location examples made it easier to compare environmental cues without treating any single branch as the main story. The outcome is a clearer picture of how visibility, surrounding businesses, and map presentation can shape customer expectations.

Project Details

  • Research Lens Location visibility, surrounding context, map presentation, and customer wayfinding.
  • Evidence Set Three equal-weight Google Maps examples reviewed as part of one research effort.
  • Outcome Focus Identify patterns that could inform branch discovery, local search presentation, and customer decision support.
